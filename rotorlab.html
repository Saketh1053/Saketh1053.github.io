Overview

RotorLab is a self-contained rotorcraft dynamics visualization: a projected 3D schematic plus top and side views of a single-rotor, counter-clockwise aircraft, with force vectors, an airflow field, and a live swashplate inset. Every control — cyclic, collective, antitorque pedals, forward groundspeed, wind, gusts, mass, density altitude, governed rotor RPM, and maneuver load factor — feeds the same model in real time, with keyboard support on every slider and the cyclic stick.

What it's modeling

The lab illustrates dissymmetry of lift between the advancing and retreating blades, the ~90° phase lag between a pitch input and the resulting flapping response, and how airspeed, gross mass, density altitude, maneuver loading, and turbulence shrink or grow the retreating side's stall margin. A built-in source register documents the five core and four supporting FAA/NASA/Army references used, along with their review scope.

This is a qualitative educational model, not validated CFD, aircraft performance software, or a flight trainer. Stall onset, angle-of-attack, and envelope cues are illustrative, and no aircraft-specific limits or recovery procedure are claimed.
