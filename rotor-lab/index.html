This is a generic educational model informed by the reviewed sources below. It is not a UH-60A/M performance model. Numerical onset, AOA, force scaling, and the 150 kt reference marker are illustrative assumptions, not published operating limits. Vne is aircraft- and condition-specific and is not identical to stall onset.

What changes the stall margin?

Higher true airspeed reduces retreating-side relative flow. Greater mass, density altitude, maneuver loading and turbulence increase demand; reduced rotor RPM decreases margin. The orange region starts near the retreating tip and spreads inward. It represents an illustrative positive-stall region, not the inboard reverse-flow or negative-lift regions.

NASA's UH-60A measurements distinguish high-loading dynamic stall, beginning on the retreating side and sometimes carrying to the advancing side, from high-speed advancing-side compressibility at moderate loading. This app does not solve dynamic-stall hysteresis, shocks, or blade/control-system torsion. The tip-Mach readout is contextual and is not a compressibility limit.

WELCOME TO ROTORLAB

Your hands on the controls.

Drag the cyclic to command disk tilt. Raise collective to increase all blade pitch and lift. Move the pedals to change yaw. Keyboard: focus the cyclic and use arrow keys; all sliders support arrow keys.

Switch between perspective, top and side views. The inset swashplate follows collective and cyclic. Green blades advance on the right; orange blades retreat on the left for this counterclockwise rotor.

Forward groundspeed is a wind-tunnel target with first-order response; cyclic adds a speed bias. Wind is relative to the aircraft nose. Lift, drag and attitude are illustrative, with a governed rotor and adjustable density altitude.

Try Stall onset to see increasing retreating-side angle of attack, pitch-up and left roll. The 150 kt marker and stall thresholds are illustrative; real Vne and stall onset depend on the aircraft and conditions.

AOA is shown at 75% span after approximate flapping compensation. Force arrows are scaled individually for readability. Rotor display is slowed while the RPM instrument shows modeled physical speed.
