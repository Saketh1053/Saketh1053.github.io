MOTOCRAFT

AI-Powered Vehicle Acquisition and Dealership Management

A dealership management platform I'm building that brings vehicle inventory, acquisitions, repairs, sales, and finances into one system, alongside an AI-assisted vehicle analysis workflow designed to help evaluate potential purchases.

Overview

MOTOCRAFT is an ongoing personal project exploring how AI can support dealership operations and vehicle purchasing decisions. The public demo is an interactive, fully client-side preview: dashboard, inventory, acquisitions, repairs, sales, and finance screens all run on fictional sample data that resets on reload, with no backend, database, or real accounts involved.

AI Vehicle Analysis

The core idea behind MOTOCRAFT is an AI-assisted analysis workflow for evaluating a vehicle before buying it. This part of the project is actively being built; the capabilities below describe the intended design rather than a finished, production-connected feature.

  • Vehicle analysis through Groq-hosted large language models, using details like make, model, mileage, price, condition, and history to produce estimated costs, risks, and a projected gross profit.
  • VIN decoding against the NHTSA vehicle information API, to cross-check entered details against known specifications.
  • Supporting document and image input (PDF, photos, DOCX, CSV/JSON) to feed additional context into an analysis.
  • Voice input using Whisper, so vehicle details can be spoken instead of typed.
  • Vehicle listing lookup by URL, to pull in advertised details and flag possible warning signs — without claiming to verify a listing or catch every scam.

AI estimates are meant to support a purchasing decision, not replace a professional inspection, and depend on the accuracy of the information supplied.

Dealership Management

Alongside the AI workflow, the demo includes a broader dealership management experience: an interactive dashboard, vehicle inventory and acquisitions, repair and parts tracking, customer and sales records, financial reports, and exportable data (PDF, Excel, CSV). A lightweight role-preview system shows how different staff views might look, without acting as real authentication.

Current Development Status

MOTOCRAFT is a work in progress. The live demo runs entirely in the browser with temporary, fictional business records and no persistence between sessions — it is not yet a production-ready system.

Planned next:

  • Persistent database storage
  • Secure, server-side authentication and role-based access control
  • Production AI integration and additional testing/validation
  • Multi-user support and secure document storage
