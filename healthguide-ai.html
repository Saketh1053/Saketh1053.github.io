HealthGuide AI

Healthcare Education Assistant

A generative AI prototype designed to explain healthcare information in simple language, with restrictions on diagnoses, prescriptions, sensitive information, and emergency handling.

  • Generative AI
  • Prompt Engineering
  • AI Safety Testing

Introduction

HealthGuide AI is a healthcare education chatbot I developed during my AI Lab. I chose this project because of my experience with EHR applications and my interest in applying AI to healthcare-related problems.

Description

The assistant explains general medical terminology, health conditions, and preventive healthcare information in simple language. It can also help users prepare questions for healthcare professionals.

HealthGuide AI is an educational prototype. It does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medication, or make treatment decisions.

Objective

My objective was to create a chatbot that provides understandable healthcare information while following specific safety and privacy restrictions.

I also wanted to evaluate how the assistant responded to different user requests and whether changes to its instructions improved its behavior.

Process

I began by identifying the intended users, educational goals, and restrictions. I then configured the first version of HealthGuide AI using ChatGPT Plugin Creator.

Version 0.1.0 was tested with questions about general healthcare education, medication safety, and role-based access control.

After reviewing the prototype, I added specific instructions for medical emergencies and mental health crises. The revised version was saved as Version 0.1.1.

I then tested additional scenarios involving diagnosis requests, sensitive patient information, uncertainty, emergencies, and mental health crisis support.

Tools and Technologies Used

ChatGPT, ChatGPT Plugin Creator, generative AI, prompt engineering, scenario-based testing, and AI response evaluation.

Testing and Results

I conducted nine tests covering the assistant's educational purpose and safety restrictions.

All nine tests met the expected behavior for the fictional scenarios used.

The tests showed that the assistant followed its main instructions during these interactions. However, the results do not establish that the prototype is suitable for real clinical use.

Value Proposition

HealthGuide AI demonstrates my ability to design an AI assistant around a defined user need, configure its behavior, evaluate its responses, and improve it through testing.

Unique Value

The project combines general healthcare education with explicit restrictions on medical decisions, personal information, uncertainty, and emergency handling.

It also includes a documented development improvement from Version 0.1.0 to Version 0.1.1.

Professional Relevance

My work on EHR applications involves testing, access control, data integrity, and reliable healthcare workflows.

HealthGuide AI allowed me to apply the same attention to requirements and safety in a generative AI prototype.
